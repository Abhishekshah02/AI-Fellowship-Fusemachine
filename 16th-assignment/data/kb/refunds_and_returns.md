# Refunds and Returns

## Return window
ShopAssist accepts returns within **30 days** of the delivery date for any item in
original condition with tags attached. Final-sale items (marked `FINAL SALE` on the
product page) and gift cards cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
1. Open **My Orders** and select the order.
2. Choose *Return or replace items*, pick a reason, and print the prepaid label.
3. Drop the parcel at any partner carrier point within 14 days of creating the label.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the warehouse scans the return.

| Payment method | Time to appear after scan |
| -------------- | ------------------------- |
| Credit / debit card | 5-7 business days |
| PayPal | 1-2 business days |
| ShopAssist store credit | Instant |
| Bank transfer | 7-10 business days |

## Partial refunds
Items returned used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts are refunded at 50%.
Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by our error (wrong item,
damaged on arrival, or late delivery beyond the guaranteed date).

## Refund not received
If the refund has not appeared after the window above, the agent should confirm the
return scan date, then escalate to the payments team with the order ID.
