# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery options
| Option | Cost | Delivery time |
| ------ | ---- | ------------- |
| Standard | Free over $50, otherwise $4.99 | 3-5 business days |
| Express | $9.99 | 1-2 business days |
| Same day (select cities) | $14.99 | Same day if ordered before 11:00 |

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse and is also shown
in **My Orders**. Tracking can take up to 6 hours to become active with the carrier.

## Late or missing parcels
- Mark a parcel as delayed only after the guaranteed delivery date has passed.
- Carriers may mark a parcel "delivered" up to 24 hours before it physically arrives.
- If a parcel is more than 3 business days late, offer a reship or a full refund.
- Parcels lost in transit are refunded in full including shipping.

## Address changes
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is `PENDING` or
`PROCESSING`. Once the status is `SHIPPED` the address is locked by the carrier and
the customer must use the carrier's own redirect service.

## International shipping
We ship to 32 countries. Duties and import taxes are collected at checkout under
Delivered Duty Paid; customers should never be asked to pay the carrier directly.
