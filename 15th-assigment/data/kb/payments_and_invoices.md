# Payments and Invoices

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ShopAssist store
credit, and bank transfer for business accounts.

## Declined payments
Common causes, in order of frequency: insufficient funds, incorrect CVV, expired
card, bank fraud block, and billing address mismatch. Ask the customer to retry with
a different method; never ask for full card numbers in chat.

## Invoices
A VAT invoice is generated when the order ships and is available in
**My Orders -> Invoice (PDF)**. Business customers can add a VAT number in account
settings; invoices issued before the VAT number is added cannot be reissued with it.

## Duplicate charges
An authorisation hold and a capture can both appear on a statement. Only the capture
is a real charge. If two captures exist for one order ID, refund the duplicate and
notify the payments team.

## Chargebacks
Once a chargeback is opened by the bank we cannot issue a refund for the same order;
the dispute must run its course. Tell the customer this plainly and escalate.
