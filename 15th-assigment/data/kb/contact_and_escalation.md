# Contact and Escalation

## Support channels
- Chat assistant: 24/7, this assistant.
- Email: support@shopassist.example, first reply within 12 hours.
- Phone: +1-555-0100, 08:00-20:00 local time, Monday to Saturday.

## When to escalate to a human agent
Create a support ticket and tell the customer a human will follow up when any of the
following is true:
- The customer asks for a human, or is angry or distressed.
- Money is disputed: duplicate charge, chargeback, or a refund past its due window.
- The account cannot be verified but the customer needs access.
- A parcel is lost, stolen, or delivered to the wrong address.
- The answer is not covered by the knowledge base.

## Ticket priorities
| Priority | Use for | First response target |
| -------- | ------- | --------------------- |
| `urgent` | Money lost, safety, legal threats | 1 hour |
| `high` | Lost parcel, account lockout | 4 hours |
| `normal` | Everything else | 12 hours |
| `low` | Feedback and feature requests | 3 business days |

## What never to do
Never promise a refund date outside the published windows, never ask for full card
numbers or passwords, and never share another customer's data.
