# Accounts and Subscriptions

## Creating and deleting an account
Accounts are created with an email and password or a Google/Apple sign-in. Deletion
is permanent and removes order history after a 30-day grace period, during which the
customer can restore the account by signing in again.

## Password and access
Password resets are sent by email and expire after 60 minutes. If the customer no
longer controls the email address, verification requires the last 4 digits of a card
used on a past order plus the order ID.

## ShopAssist Plus subscription
$7.99 per month or $79 per year. Includes free express shipping, a 60-day return
window instead of 30, and early access to sales.

## Managing the subscription
Cancel any time in **Account -> Subscriptions**; benefits continue until the end of
the paid period and no pro-rata refund is given for monthly plans. Annual plans
cancelled within 14 days of purchase are refunded in full.

## Failed subscription renewal
We retry a failed renewal on days 1, 3, and 7. After the third failure the
subscription lapses and the account returns to the standard tier.
